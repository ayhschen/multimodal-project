The tradeoffs and open questions
Where AI-assisted creativity gets complicated
Tradeoff
Less originality
Hover to explore
Since models are trained on stuff that already exists, they mostly just mix up old patterns. This means the results can feel a bit generic instead of actually being new.
Tradeoff
Homogenization risk
Hover to explore
If everyone is using the same tools to get their work done, we might lose out on the unique styles and different voices that make things interesting.
Concern
Who owns the work?
Hover to explore
Copyright laws haven't really caught up to this tech yet. It creates a lot of confusion over who gets the credit or gets paid for work made with AI.
Concern
Shift in human agency
Hover to explore
If we let AI handle all the hard parts of being creative, we might stop developing the skills that come from struggling through a tough project ourselves.
